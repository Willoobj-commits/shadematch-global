Privacy Policy

ShadeMatch Global · Last updated 1 September 2026

Applies to ShadeMatch Global for Android (com.shadematchglobal.shade_match_global) and iOS (com.shadematchglobal.shadematchglobal).

ShadeMatch Global does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app works entirely offline. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, photos, camera access, contacts, or any device identifier. No information about you or your device leaves your phone.

What is stored on your device

The shades you save are stored on your device only, as a list of shade identifiers (for example shd_2f169ed1bea844ac). This list never leaves your device and is not readable by us or anyone else. It is not backed up to any service we operate.

Deleting the app removes this list permanently. You can also clear it at any time from within the app.

The shade catalogue

The catalogue of 1,578 shades across 34 matchable brands ships inside the app as a bundled data file. Searching, browsing, and matching all run on your device. The app makes no network requests to retrieve or update the catalogue, and sends no record of what you search for or match.

Links to other websites

Each shade lists the manufacturer or retailer page it was sourced from. Opening one of these links hands the address to your device's browser and leaves ShadeMatch Global. We do not control those sites, and this policy does not cover them — once you follow a link, the privacy policy of the site you land on applies.

Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or measurement SDK, and no crash-reporting service. No advertising identifier is read or used.

Children

ShadeMatch Global is a cosmetics reference tool intended for a general audience. It is not directed at children, and because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins collecting data — for example, if optional cloud sync for saved shades is added — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here with a new "last updated" date.
